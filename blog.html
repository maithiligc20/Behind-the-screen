What Happens Behind the Screen When You Click?

Every click starts a journey. Discover how your browser, the internet, and a server work together to bring a website to your screen.

Technology Explained Simply

Have you ever wondered what happens when you click a link and a website opens almost instantly? There is an entire process happening behind the scenes.

01. Your browser receives the click

When you click a website link, your web browser, such as Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome, identifies the address of the page you want to visit.

The browser then prepares to contact the computer that stores the website.

02. The internet finds the server

Websites are stored on computers called servers. A server can receive requests and send information back to the browser.

The Domain Name System, or DNS, helps translate a website's domain name into an IP address so that the browser can locate the correct destination.

03. A request travels across the internet

Your browser sends an HTTP or HTTPS request to the website's server. This request asks for the page and the resources needed to display it.

HTTPS also helps protect information exchanged between your browser and the website.

04. The server sends a response

The server receives the request and sends back a response. This may include HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images, and other information.

HTML provides the page's structure, CSS controls its appearance, and JavaScript can make it interactive.

05. Your website appears

Your browser processes the received files and combines the structure, design, and necessary scripts to display the website.

That's why a page can appear on your screen within seconds. Your click has started a conversation between your browser and another computer.

The invisible world is fascinating

Something as simple as clicking a link involves several technologies working together. Understanding these processes helps us appreciate the engineering behind the digital world.

Next time you open a website, remember: there is much more happening than meets the eye.
